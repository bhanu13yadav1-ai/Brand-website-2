BETTER INFORMED, BETTER CHOICES

Details worth checking.

These general reminders help make the future catalogue more useful. Follow the instructions provided for each real product and seek professional advice for specific health concerns.

✳

Materials first

Look for clear material disclosures and reliable manufacturer information. Avoid items with unknown coatings or vague material descriptions.

⌁

Cleaning & storage

Check whether a product is waterproof or splash-resistant rather than assuming. Use compatible cleaners and follow the maker's storage instructions.

♡

Consent & boundaries

With a partner, discuss comfort, boundaries and a clear pause/stop signal. Either person can change their mind at any time.
